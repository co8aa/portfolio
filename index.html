Case 01 · Retention
Goal

Reduce revenue churn at the cancellation step and win back users who are still open to staying.

Context

About €500K of revenue per month was lost to churn. One generic cancellation flow offered every user the same thing, regardless of reason or actual product usage.

Role

Strategy, flow design, segmentation logic, metrics and experiment design.

What was done

Designed a 4-step flow: behavioural scoring → reason survey → offer matched to reason × usage segment (usage-based plan, discount, free plan, pause, data export) → graceful exit with follow-ups. Rule-based first, then a Multi-Armed Bandit once each segment has enough data.

Case 02 · Monetization
Goal

Reduce dependence on ad revenue and build predictable recurring revenue without losing developer trust.

Context

A global platform earned ~90% of revenue from ads: volatile CPMs, heavy ad load hurting first sessions, and developers distrusting revenue attribution.

Role

Product strategy, monetization model, MVP scoping, roadmap.

What was done

Built a hypothesis-driven roadmap with Go/No-Go gates: ad-free subscription MVP in 2 months (ad-removal layer, no developer work), a two-region pilot with a price A/B test, then a coin wallet with a 70/30 IAP split, then Premium+ or a seasonal pass. Legal and developer revenue-share covered.
